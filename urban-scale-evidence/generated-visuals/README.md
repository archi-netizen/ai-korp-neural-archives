# Urban-Scale Evidence: Generated Visuals

This folder contains AI-generated schematic visuals illustrating the three 
brand-architecture fusion types identified in the aerial imagery survey 
documented in the main repository README.

All visuals are generated using Google Gemini Imagen (2026). No real brand 
trade dress, logo, colour system, or typographic identity is reproduced. 
Fictional colour systems are used throughout to illustrate the fusion 
mechanism without reference to any specific company.

---

## Purpose

These visuals serve as Figure 4 in the associated preprint:

**Mate, K. (2026). Sorting the Harvest at Scale: A Governance Framework 
for Generative AI, from Brand Systems to Urban Futures. Zenodo. 
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21460708**

The paper describes Figure 4 as:
> *Schematic (not photographic, to sidestep trademark-image-reproduction 
> issues) of a generic branded logistics corridor: storefront, distribution 
> centre, delivery vehicle, and hoarding sharing one colour system.*

These images fulfil that function across all three fusion types.

## Fusion Type Illustrations

### Type A — Fleet-carried brand signal, neutral envelope
**File:** `TypeA_fleet_carried.png`

A generic logistics distribution centre with a neutral grey building envelope. 
Brand signal carried entirely by delivery vehicles, trailers, and loading 
equipment in a unified fictional colour. The building itself is unbranded. 
Aerial and street-level views.

**Fictional colour used:** Deep teal `#1A6B6B`
**Prompt basis:** Aerial view of a large logistics shed, grey envelope, 
all fleet vehicles in unified deep teal, no logos or text, photorealistic.

---

### Type B — Envelope-carried brand signal
**File:** `TypeB_envelope_carried.png`

A generic logistics or retail facility where the building facade, roof, and 
external cladding carry the brand signal at architectural scale. Readable 
from aerial altitude without any fleet present. No logos or text visible — 
colour alone carries the identification.

**Fictional colour used:** Burnt sienna `#C45E2A`
**Prompt basis:** Aerial view of a large retail warehouse, entire building 
facade and roof in unified burnt sienna, no fleet visible, no logos or text, 
photorealistic, urban edge location.

---

### Type C — Hybrid
**File:** `TypeC_hybrid.png`

A facility combining genuine architectural ambition in the envelope — 
distinctive material treatment, considered massing — with a brand mark 
applied at urban-legible scale. The building reads simultaneously as 
architecture and brand infrastructure.

**Fictional colour used:** Slate blue `#3D5A80` with a simplified geometric 
mark applied to the facade.
**Prompt basis:** Aerial view of an architecturally designed logistics centre, 
distinctive perforated concrete facade, slate blue brand mark at building 
scale, no text or recognisable logo, photorealistic.
